02 The Problem

Solar Operations Are Broken

Utility-scale farms deploy thousands of inverters. When one degrades, operators drown in raw telemetry they can't interpret.

⚠

Alarm Fatigue

SCADA systems push hundreds of discrete metrics at 5-minute intervals. Subtle pre-fault signatures are buried under routine data noise.

🔎

Black-Box AI

ML models output risk percentages and SHAP log-odds. Field technicians lack context to operationalize these data-science outputs.

📄

Manual Ticket Writing

Maintenance tickets are written by hand, slow and error-prone. No automated triage; same failures recur across fleet.

🌐

No Unified View

No centralized monitoring across multiple plants, blocks, and inverters. Siloed data prevents fleet-wide pattern recognition.

04 Our Solution

End-to-End Intelligence Platform

Four independently deployable microservices covering the full telemetry-to-action pipeline.

🤖

ML Pipeline

7-stage pipeline: SCADA ingestion, 183 engineered features, SMOTE balancing, Optuna-tuned XGBoost, SHAP explainability. Predicts no_risk, degradation_risk, and shutdown_risk.

⚡

ML Inference Server

FastAPI real-time prediction API. Single <50ms, batch <200ms. Per-prediction SHAP bar charts, 5-category mapping (A-E), contextual fault descriptions.

💡

Web Application
